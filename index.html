About Me

Building the future,
one commit at a time.

I'm Sabarinathan Ragunathan, a Software Development Engineer at M2P Fintech in Chennai. I build the backend of lending products: the loan origination, management and credit line systems that have to be right every single time. My tools are Java and Spring Boot, Node.js, Golang, and REST APIs designed to scale. B.Tech in Computer Science from SRM University.

I like hard problems and short feedback loops. Give me a legacy system to modernise, a production issue to trace to its root, or a new intern to bring up to speed, and I'm at my best. Outside work I keep learning, mostly for the fun of it.
